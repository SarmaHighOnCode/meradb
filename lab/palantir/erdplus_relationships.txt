Relationships to draw by hand in ERDPlus (the free trial editor has no Relationship tool).
Format: parent  (cardinality)  child   -- foreign key

departments   1 : N  employees     employees.dept_id    -> departments.dept_id   (a department has many employees; an employee may have none)
employees     1 : N  employees     employees.manager_id -> employees.emp_id      (recursive: a manager manages many)
clients       1 : N  contracts     contracts.client_id  -> clients.client_id     (mandatory on the contract)
products      1 : N  contracts     contracts.product_id -> products.product_id   (mandatory on the contract)
contracts     1 : N  projects      projects.contract_id -> contracts.contract_id (optional: project 406 has none)
employees     1 : N  projects      projects.lead_emp    -> employees.emp_id      (optional)
employees     M : N  projects      through assignments (emp_id, project_id), attributes role, hours
